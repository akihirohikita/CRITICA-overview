# CRITICA

**Structure, Not Culture.**  
An AI Knowledge Infrastructure for capturing, structuring, preserving, and transferring field knowledge, tacit expertise, and operational know-how.

CRITICA is designed to help organizations convert scattered documents, multimedia records, expert conversations, daily observations, and improvement proposals into structured, searchable, and continuously improving organizational knowledge.

---

## What CRITICA Does

CRITICA links knowledge to operational structures such as:

- Framework
- Phase
- Category
- Process
- Step

This allows AI to answer questions not only from documents, but from knowledge that is tied to the actual structure of work.

---

## Core Concept

CRITICA is not just a RAG chatbot.

It is a knowledge infrastructure that supports:

- Field knowledge capture
- Tacit knowledge preservation
- Process-based knowledge structuring
- Human-in-the-loop knowledge approval
- AI-assisted search and response
- Knowledge correction and improvement
- Organizational learning visualization

---

## Key Features

- Multi-tenant knowledge management
- Process / step-based knowledge structure
- RAG-based AI chat
- Human Expert Query workflow
- KAIZEN proposal workflow
- Daily reflection and operational logging
- Telegram-based field knowledge capture concept
- Knowledge correction flow
- Knowledge Relevance Calibration
- Knowledge Emergence Graph
